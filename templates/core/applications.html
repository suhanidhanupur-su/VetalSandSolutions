Key applications

Industrial uses shaped by performance and process requirements.

01

Foundry & Casting

Silica sand is commonly used in foundry and casting environments where moulding and process stability are important.

02

Glass Manufacturing

Silica sand is a core material in glass production, supporting the composition requirements of many glassmaking processes.

03

Construction & Masonry

In construction and masonry work, silica sand contributes to material performance in products and building applications.

04

Water Filtration

Silica sand is widely used as a granular filter media in water treatment and filtration systems that require reliable media performance.

05

Ceramics

Silica sand supports ceramic production where material consistency and formulation suitability are important to the final product.

Grade selection

Choosing the Right Grade

Selecting the right silica sand grade depends on the intended industrial application, the material specifications required, and the processing conditions involved. Different grades may be better suited to different operational needs, so grade choice should be guided by functional requirements and application fit.

The project already references the following grades for general product discussions: 16/30, 24/40, 45/50, 50/60, and 60/70. These grades may be considered as part of broader product selection discussions, depending on the specific industrial use case.
